How the Tracker Works

  • Everyone who submits a form on the website is collated online in one central Google Sheet (your tracker) with status Pending.
  • You alone approve: click ✓ Approve or Reject on any record — the decision is saved to the Sheet and included in CSV exports.
  • This dashboard reads that Sheet live — same numbers on your phone, laptop or any device.
  • A copy of every submission is also emailed to Dynastyofhope2023@gmail.com.
  • Use the CSV buttons for Excel backups any time.
