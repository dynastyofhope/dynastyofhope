Sponsorship Proposal

Charity Carnival Blast & Mega Raffle Draw — Sunday, 27th December 2026

1. Introduction

Dynasty of Hope Foundation cordially invites your organisation to partner with us as a sponsor of the Charity Carnival Blast & Mega Raffle Draw — a full day of games, music, food, family fun and big raffle prizes, holding on Sunday, 27th December 2026 at the Goni Gora Government Primary School, U/Bijeh, Kaduna.

The carnival is the grand finale of our December 2026 community programme, immediately following the Gonigora Community Development Football Championship (1–26 December). It draws the whole community together — players, families, traders, community leaders and guests — to celebrate, give and win.

2. About the Foundation

Dynasty of Hope Foundation is a Nigerian non-governmental organisation committed to community development through free health outreach, education support, food relief and youth empowerment programmes. Since 2023 we have served hundreds of families in Gonigora and neighbouring communities with the help of over 80 active volunteers.

4. Why Sponsor the Carnival

  • Festive, high-traffic audience — families and youth in a celebratory mood, perfect for brand sampling and goodwill.
  • Stage & MC visibility — your brand announced live during games, performances and every raffle draw round.
  • Print & digital reach — logo on posters, tickets, stage backdrop and our social media campaign.
  • Raffle association — sponsor a prize and your brand is literally in the winner's hands (and photos).
  • Documented CSR — a feel-good community story for your annual report and media channels.

6. Stalls & Raffle Opportunities

  • Vendor stalls: limited food, craft and merchandise stalls are available for booking — early sponsors receive priority stall placement.
  • Raffle ticket bulk buyers: organisations may purchase ticket blocks for staff, customers or community giveaway, with brand mention at the draw.
  • Adopt-a-game: sponsor a specific family game or children's activity and brand it in your name.
