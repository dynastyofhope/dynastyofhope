Event 2 · Sunday, 27 December 2026

Charity Carnival Blast & Mega Raffle Draw

  • Date: Sunday, 27th December 2026
  • Venue: Goni Gora Government Primary School, U/Bijeh, Kaduna
  • Highlights: Games, live music, food stalls, children's fun fair & the Mega Raffle Draw

One glorious day to close the year: family games, performances by local artists, food and craft stalls — and our Mega Raffle Draw with big prizes to be won. Every ticket and stall fee funds community development, education and health outreach in Gonigora.

🎟 Raffle & Entry

Raffle tickets are on sale now via the registration desk, our WhatsApp line and volunteer coordinators. Come with family and friends — entry is free with a raffle ticket.
