Sponsorship Proposal

Gonigora Community Development Football Championship Tournament, Kaduna — 1st to 26th December 2026

Looking for the carnival? → Charity Carnival Blast & Mega Raffle Draw Sponsorship Proposal

1. Introduction

Dynasty of Hope Foundation cordially invites your esteemed organisation to partner with us as a sponsor of the Gonigora Community Development Football Championship Tournament, a month-long community football competition holding from 1st December to 26th December 2026 at the Gonigora Government Primary School, Ungwa Bijeh, Kaduna.

The tournament brings together youth clubs from communities across Kaduna for daily matches, drawing crowds of families, traders, community leaders and local media — and closing with an award ceremony and the Charity Carnival Blast on 27th December 2026.

2. About the Foundation

Dynasty of Hope Foundation is a Nigerian non-governmental organisation committed to community development through free health outreach, education support, food relief and youth empowerment programmes. Since 2023 we have served hundreds of families in Gonigora and neighbouring communities with the help of over 80 active volunteers.

4. Why Sponsor This Tournament

  • Massive community visibility — your brand in front of thousands of spectators across 26 match days.
  • Positive association — link your name to youth development, peace-building and community pride.
  • Media & digital reach — logo and mentions on fliers, jerseys, banners and our social media campaigns.
  • Direct engagement — brand stands, product sampling and goodwill addresses at the venue.
  • Measurable goodwill — a documented CSR story you can publish in your annual report.

6. Award Categories

Beyond the champions' cup, the tournament honours: Best Player of the Tournament, Top Scorer (Golden Boot), Best Goalkeeper, Most Disciplined Team, Fair Play Award, Coach of the Tournament, Match Officials' Award and the Community Impact Award. Sponsors may adopt any award category for additional branding.
